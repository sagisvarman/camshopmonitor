How it works

You keep washing bikes. The camera keeps count.

We do the setup. After that there is nothing for you or your staff to press.

  1. We connect to your camera

    We play your camera's live view on our side: the same picture you see in the DMSS app.

  2. We mark your wash mats

    Each mat becomes a zone. A bike that stops on a zone counts as a wash. A bike parked anywhere else counts as off the mats.

  3. Every bike is logged

    Arrival time, departure time, time on the mat, colour, and the plate when it can be read. A bike that comes back within 30 minutes stays one visit.

  4. You open your report

    On your phone, any time of day. Sign in with Google or with the email and password we give you.

Questions

Before you ask

Do I need a new camera?

No. If you can watch your wash area in the DMSS app today, we can count from that camera. It should see the whole of each mat from above or from the side.

Does it read every number plate?

No. It reads a plate when the camera sees it clearly enough. A bike whose plate can't be read is still counted, with its colour and times.

What counts as a wash?

A bike that stops inside one of your mat zones. A bike that parks outside the zones is listed as off the mats, so it doesn't inflate your wash count.

How do I get a login?

We create it when we set up your shop. You get an email and password, or we add your Google account. Then press Sign in on this page.

Which languages does the report have?

English and Bahasa Indonesia. Switch at the top of the report at any time.
